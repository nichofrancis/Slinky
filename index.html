Emergent Simulators

Complex behaviour arising from simple rules. Click any simulator to explore.

Cellular Automata
Conway's Game of Life
Cells live, die, and reproduce based on their neighbours. One of the most famous examples of emergence.
Swarm Intelligence
Boids Flocking
Hundreds of agents follow three simple rules — separation, alignment, cohesion — and produce lifelike flocking.
Stigmergy
Ant Colony
Ants deposit pheromone trails to communicate. Watch shortest paths emerge with no central planning.
Chemistry
Reaction-Diffusion
The Gray-Scott model produces leopard spots, coral patterns, and labyrinths from two simple chemical reactions.
Biological
Slime Mold
Physarum-inspired agents sense chemical gradients and build efficient transport networks.
Spreading Dynamics
Forest Fire
Trees randomly ignite and spread flames to neighbours. Percolation thresholds and firebreaks emerge naturally.
Particle Physics
Falling Sand
Sand, water, and fire particles interact through simple local rules, producing avalanches and fluid dynamics.
Social Dynamics
Schelling Segregation
Agents with mild preferences for similar neighbours produce stark large-scale segregation — a social emergence model.
